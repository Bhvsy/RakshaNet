How it works

From a suspicious message to a safe decision in three taps.

Fraud works by rushing people. RakshaNet slows the moment down and tells you exactly what to do.

1

Paste or snap it

Drop in a link, phone number, UPI ID or SMS, or upload a screenshot of the message or payment QR.

2

Gemini AI checks it

You get a 0 to 100 risk score and a plain-language reason in your own language, cross-checked with the community blacklist.

3

Act with protection

A cooling-off timer slows risky payments, family guardians get alerted, and 1930 is one tap away.

How to install the APK

  1. 1

    Download the file

    Tap Download APK on your Android phone. If Chrome says "This type of file can harm your device", tap Download anyway. Android shows this for every app installed outside the Play Store.

  2. 2

    Open the downloaded file

    Tap Open in the download notification, or go to Files → Downloads and tap RakshaNet.apk.

  3. 3

    Allow installs from this source

    If Android asks, tap Settings and switch on Allow from this source for Chrome or Files, then press back. You can find it again under Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps.

  4. 4

    Tap Install

    Tap Install. If Google Play Protect shows a warning about an unrecognized app, tap More details → Install anyway.

  5. 5

    Open RakshaNet

    Tap Open, choose English, Hindi or Haryanvi, and your shield is ready.

Installation not working?
  • "App not installed": free up some storage, and uninstall any older RakshaNet version first.
  • Can't tap Install: make sure step 3 is enabled for the app you opened the file from.
  • Download didn't start: use "View file on GitHub" above and tap the download icon there.
